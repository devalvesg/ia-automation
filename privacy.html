Privacy Policy

IA-Automation · Last updated: 18 October 2026

IA-Automation is a personal automation operated by a single independent developer for their own TikTok Shop affiliate account. It has no users other than its operator: there is no sign-up, no account system, and no way for anyone else to connect an account to it.

Because of that, this policy describes what the software stores on the operator's own machine — not data collected from the public.

What is stored

DataWhyWhere
TikTok access and refresh tokens To upload videos to the operator's own drafts A local configuration file readable only by the operating-system user (permissions 600)
Basic TikTok profile info (user.info.basic) To confirm which account was authorized Held in memory during a run; not written to disk
Public product data (name, price, sales figures, product photo) To choose a product and write the script A local SQLite database on the operator's machine
Generated videos and scripts The output of the tool Local files on the operator's machine

What is not done

  • No personal data is collected from any third party.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies are used on this website.
  • No TikTok data is used for any purpose other than uploading the operator's own drafts.
  • Nothing is published to TikTok automatically — every video waits in drafts for a human.

Third-party services

Scripts are written and videos are generated by third-party AI providers. Only the product description and product photo are sent to them. No TikTok credentials and no TikTok profile data are ever sent to any third party.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use. The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's own settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions); revoking immediately invalidates the stored tokens. Deleting the local configuration file removes them from disk. Generated videos and product data are deleted by removing the local files and database.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is published on this page with a new date.
