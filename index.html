IA-Automation

A personal tool that drafts short promotional videos for TikTok Shop products.

IA-Automation is a single-operator automation built and run by an independent TikTok Shop affiliate. It prepares short vertical videos about products the operator promotes and places them in the operator's own TikTok drafts, where they are reviewed and published by hand.

It is not a public service. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no third-party users. The only TikTok account it ever touches is the operator's own.

How it works

  1. It reads a public best-seller ranking of TikTok Shop products and picks one product per day.
  2. It writes a short script and a shot description for that product, using a text model.
  3. It generates one vertical video clip from the product photo, using a video model.
  4. It uploads the finished video to the operator's TikTok drafts through the Content Posting API.
  5. The operator opens TikTok, reviews the draft, attaches the affiliate product, adds the caption and disclosures, and publishes — or discards it.

How TikTok is used

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic) — to confirm which account the authorization belongs to, so a video is never sent to the wrong profile.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload) — to place the video in the operator's drafts. Direct Post is switched off.

Nothing is ever published automatically. Every video reaches TikTok as a draft and stays there until a person decides what to do with it. Videos are generated with AI and are declared as such when published.
