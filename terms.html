1. What this is

IA-Automation is a private, single-operator tool that prepares short promotional videos about TikTok Shop products and places them in its operator's own TikTok drafts. It is not offered to the public, sold, licensed, or made available as a service. There are no users other than the operator.

2. Use of TikTok

The tool uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API under the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload, against the operator's own account and with the operator's own authorization. Direct Post is disabled: content is uploaded only to drafts.

Use of this tool is subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and developer policies. Where anything here conflicts with those, TikTok's terms prevail.

3. Human review before publication

Nothing reaches a public profile automatically. Every generated video stops in drafts and is published only after a person reviews it.

At publication the operator is responsible for attaching the correct affiliate product, for the caption, and for enabling the required disclosures — including the AI-generated-content label and the commercial-content disclosure where applicable.

4. AI-generated content

Scripts and video are produced by AI models. Videos are generated from product photographs supplied by sellers and are labelled as AI-generated when published. The tool does not attempt to recreate the likeness of any real identifiable person; where a supplier photograph shows an identifiable face, the tool stops and requires manual intervention.

5. Accuracy and responsibility

Product claims shown in a generated video originate from public marketplace listings and are not independently verified. The operator checks each draft before publishing and is solely responsible for the content that goes live.
